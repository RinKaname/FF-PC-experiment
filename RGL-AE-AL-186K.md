# RGL-AE-AL-186K: Reconstruction-guided Local Autoencoder with Attractor Logic

**Model Repository:** [RinKana/RGL-AE-AL-186K](https://huggingface.co/RinKana/RGL-AE-AL-186K)

## Overview
The RGL-AE-AL-186K is an experimental 64x64 Anime Face Generator and feature extractor built on a completely custom hybrid training architecture. It discards traditional end-to-end backpropagation across the entire network in favor of layer-by-layer local learning.

The "AL" in the name stands for **Attractor Logic**, a nod to the fact that the network's Forward-Forward objective function does not optimize towards a zero-error state, but rather stabilizes into a mathematically balanced attractor field (the **FF Equilibrium**).

## Architectural Design

The model is built on two conflicting but complementary systems:

### 1. The Bottom-Up Encoder (PyTorch Autograd)
The encoder consists of three local 2D convolutional layers. Unlike standard VAEs, there is no global loss. Each layer is greedily trained using a modified **Forward-Forward (FF)** algorithm.
- **Positive Data:** Real anime faces.
- **Negative Data:** Brutally scrambled anime faces (2x2 non-overlapping patch destruction mixed with random Gaussian static) to destroy spatial coherence without changing color histograms.
- **FF Equilibrium:** The layers use a relative margin to maximize the Goodness (negative MSE) of real data while minimizing the Goodness of corrupted data. The network inherently flatlines at an equilibrium point (~2.16) where the forces of reconstruction and corruption rejection are perfectly balanced.

### 2. The Top-Down Decoder (Pure Numba CUDA Kernels)
The generative pathway is implemented via entirely custom top-down predictive coding kernels written in pure Numba CUDA (e.g., `conv_karl_update_kernel`).
- It attempts to reconstruct the lower-level representations ($x_{true}$) from the higher-level latents ($z_{current}$).
- By enforcing this strict top-down reconstruction at every local step, the network learns a highly structured, semantically meaningful latent space without a global decoder.

## Training Phases

The model weights evolved through three distinct training phases on mixed anime datasets (a combination of a specific character dataset, Ganyu, and a general high-quality anime portrait dataset). This curriculum approach helped prevent catastrophic forgetting and overfitting.

### Phase 1: The Initialization Phase (20 Epochs)
- **Dataset:** 2,850 Mixed Anime Images.
- **Objective:** Establish the primary feature extractors and stabilize the FF Equilibrium.
- **Result:** The `mse_pos` dropped from ~0.1540 to ~0.0480, resulting in near-perfect 64x64 reconstructions. The `FF Equilibrium` metric successfully locked into its steady state at ~2.1620.

### Phase 2: The Stabilization Phase (1 Epoch)
- **Dataset:** 2,850 Mixed Anime Images (Same distribution).
- **Objective:** Fine-tune the local routing algorithms and solidify the attractor field after the primary learning rate decay.
- **Result:** Ensured that the model did not experience parameter explosion, maintaining the razor-sharp color accuracy established in Phase 1.

### Phase 3: The Generalization Phase (1 Epoch)
- **Dataset:** 3,850 Mixed Anime Images (Expanded distribution).
- **Objective:** Expose the stabilized latent space to a wider variety of anime facial geometries to ensure the Numba CUDA top-down decoder can generalize beyond its initial constraints.
- **Result:** The final weights saved to the Hugging Face repository. The model is highly robust to variations in eye shape, hair color, and facial orientation.

## Future Potential: The Attractor Logic Diffuser
Because the RGL-AE-AL-186K provides a highly robust, compressed representation of anime faces via local predictive coding, it is the perfect base model for a Latent Diffusion approach. The `z3_pos` latent space generated by this network can be frozen and used to train a tiny denoising network, completely bypassing the need for a massive VAE.
